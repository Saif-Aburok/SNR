Seif, 18, from Khan Yunis, writes from Gaza for his family of seven.

My story

I am Seif, from Khan Yunis. I am 18 years old, and this is what happened to my family.

01

Before the War

Our house was small and our life was simple. We knew poverty and we knew the siege, and still we held on to the days we had. I worked my own small farm to feed my family, and every hard day was ours, and we were together.

02

Seven of Us

We are seven: my three sisters, my two brothers, my mother and my father. Through everything that came, we stayed together. Whatever we had to carry, and whichever road we were forced to take, we carried it as one family.

03

Leaving, Again and Again

After October 7, everything turned upside down. We were forced to leave our home and flee, looking for a safe place, and we never found one. We were displaced more than five times, between Khan Yunis, Mawasi and Rafah, and the bombing followed us everywhere. Death seemed to walk behind us wherever we went. I lost many friends and relatives. Some were killed in the bombing; others died of hunger, disease, and the lack of medicine and treatment.

04

Among the Tents

Our house near the border, east of Khan Yunis, was hit by shells and missiles, and large parts of it burned. My farm was destroyed by the bombing and the tanks. Now the house lies inside the buffer zone, and returning means risking our lives. So we live in worn-out tents of cloth and nylon, under the burning sun in summer, and in the rain and storms of winter.

05

What I Am Asking

I am asking for help so that my family can live a simple life: food, water, clothes and medicine, which have become expensive and hard to find. We need to replace our worn tents, or rent a small house to shelter us from the winter cold. Please donate, and tell others our story, so that we do not lose hope and can rebuild our lives.
